# Supermarket Retail Sales & Customer Behavior Analysis

An Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) project analyzing retail transaction patterns, product category performance, digital payment adoption, and customer ticket size using **Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn**.

## Key Findings from the Analysis
- **Top Revenue Category:** **Beverages** generated the highest overall sales revenue, followed by **Personal Care**, **Dairy**, and **Grocery**.
- **Digital Payment Dominance:** **UPI** and **Net Banking** accounted for the highest transaction frequency compared to Card and Cash payments.
- **Customer Segment Ticket Size:** **Member** customers recorded a slightly higher average basket value (~₹482) compared to **Normal** walk-in customers (~₹468).

## Actionable Business Recommendations
1. **Inventory & Merchandising:** Prioritize stock availability for high-velocity categories (**Beverages** and **Personal Care**) and pair them with slower-moving categories (**Bakery** and **Vegetables**) via combo offers.
2. **Digital Checkout Incentives:** Leverage high **UPI** adoption by introducing threshold cashback offers (e.g., on orders above ₹500) to increase average basket size.
3. **Loyalty Program Targeting:** Introduce tiered rewards for repeat **Normal** customers to convert them into high-retention **Members**.
